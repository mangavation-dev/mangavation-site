## Intro

The number one theory revolving around [All Might](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Toshinori_Yagi) is, is All Might turning from his Small Might form to his All Might form a [Quirk](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Quirk)? Many people accept the idea that just because All Might is [Quirkless](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Quirkless), him transforming from his Small Might form to his All Might form is something that should not be emphasized at all. But I would beg to differ.

One of the first things we learned about All Might that distinguished him from [Deku](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Izuku_Midoriya) is that he was able to use [One For All](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/One_For_All) from the jump because he had a special body. Those were the words of [Gran Torino](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Sorahiko_Torino) himself. And not only is there a visual difference between the Small Might form and the All Might form, in episode 1 of My Hero Academia he literally was in his Small Might form and no one thought that he was All Might. So visually, in the story, narratively, they're two different people.

But the main feat, if you will, that really spearheaded this idea of All Might's body being just different is his fight against [All For One](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/All_For_One). In particular, there's one specific scene where All Might has a buff left arm, and when All For One tries to attack him, he literally transfers the buff left arm to his buff right arm, as if he's Popeye. There's no way I can unsee the fact that All Might's body, for some reason, is just weird. It allows him to do things that nobody else is able to do, nobody besides one person.

Listen to me when I say this: All Might has surpassed the [Singularity](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Quirk_Singularity). But in thinking about it, I don't know if you would agree with this conclusion. You need some groundwork, you need some context as to how I came to this conclusion from a different conclusion. So I'm going to postpone that, and I'm going to tell you something about the Quirk Singularity.

## The Quirk Singularity

The Quirk Singularity is a theory that was created, or rather founded and popularized, by [Kyudai Garaki](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Kyudai_Garaki), which is Dr. Ujiko, the same doctor that is working with the [League of Villains](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/League_of_Villains) and All For One. The theory is based off of Dr. Ujiko's observation of the Quirks that are generated from generation to generation. He noticed that whenever two people mate and conceive a child, the child's Quirk consists of one of three different options.

The first option is one of the parents' Quirks, so characters like [Mirio](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Mirio_Togata) who got the same Quirk as his father. Option number two is a combination of both of your parents' Quirks, like [Bakugo](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuki_Bakugo), who has a combination of his mother's nitroglycerin skin and his father's acid sweat, which, when combined, creates a new Quirk, [Explosion](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Explosion). And the third option, which is extremely rare, is having both of your parents' Quirks. Currently there's only one person that I'm tracking in the entire story of My Hero Academia that has both of his parents' Quirks, and we know that that is [Shoto](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_Todoroki).

Observing the generations and generations of Quirks that are being manifested in the world, Dr. Ujiko also noticed that with every newly manifested Quirk, the power of the Quirk was significantly stronger than that of their parents. So he made a theory that says with every generation of Quirk users, the Quirk will get stronger, and eventually there will come a time where the Quirks are so strong that the person with the Quirk will be incapable of actually using it. All Dr. Ujiko was trying to do was warn everybody about this phenomenon, that it will eventually happen, but no one wanted to believe him. No one outside of All For One.

When All For One picked up on Dr. Ujiko's theory, he took that information, and from that point on decided to create a plan that would allow him to supersede the future events of the Quirk Singularity. It was a plan that would eventually end, temporarily, when he meets All Might.

## The Missing Piece

Dr. Ujiko, although he is right in his assessment and observation on the Quirk Singularity Theory, there's one ginormous thing that Dr. Ujiko never considered. His theory is only based on the idea that as the Quirks evolve throughout generations, the biological structure, or the body and genetics, of the Quirk user is not evolving as well. So if by chance we can identify an evolution of the biological structure of humans in My Hero Academia that is separate from the evolution of the manifestation of Quirks, then that would essentially invalidate Dr. Ujiko's otherwise incomplete Quirk Singularity Theory.

A good way to visualize what I'm saying is to have two charts. One line shows the growth, evolution, and power of Quirks from generation to generation. The other line shows the evolution of the biological structure of a basic human from generation to generation. And this is where I'm going to blow your mind, I hope.

There are characters that are perfect examples for the evolution of the physical structure of humans that aren't affected by their Quirks, meaning their Quirk has no effect on their physical mutation. Two of the best examples in My Hero Academia are [Fumikage Tokoyami](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Fumikage_Tokoyami) and [Koji Koda](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Koji_Koda) from [Class 1-A](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Class_1-A). Both of these characters are visibly mutated beings. Yes, they are humans, but Tokoyami literally has a bird head, and Koda visibly has a rock head, I don't even know what it is. But the point of emphasis is that Tokoyami has the Quirk [Dark Shadow](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Shadow), which essentially allows him to emit a sentient shadow from himself that he's able to control, and Koda has the Quirk [Anivoice](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Anivoice), which allows him to control and speak with any animal in the world.

Now, if you haven't noticed by now, Dark Shadow and Anivoice are not [Mutant-type Quirks](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Mutant_Quirks), meaning the Quirk didn't create the mutation that they visibly have on their body. That is a very important thing when we're looking at how one gets their Quirk in the first place. In seeing that Koda was born with the body that he had from his mom, it's fair to say that Tokoyami's mom or dad likely have a bird head. And because that is true, this is where my parallel position on the Quirk Singularity Theory comes true.

Just as, through generation and generation, the Quirks are evolving, through generation and generation the biological structure of humans is also evolving. If it is accurate to say that the biological structure of humans is evolving with each generation, albeit at a slower pace, and the Quirks are evolving with each generation, what that does mean is that eventually there will come a time in which humans will be able to equip the powers that are superseding their bodies. That will eventually negate the Quirk Singularity Theory completely. But guess what, All For One already knew this, and it's proven by the main plan he's been orchestrating over the past few decades, which is to find a body strong enough to genetically modify, to supersede the evolution of Quirks, or to supersede the Quirk Singularity.

## All Might Surpassed the Singularity

But what if what All For One is trying to do artificially, by surgically creating a body able to surpass the Quirk Singularity, what if someone was actually born into the world of My Hero Academia already having surpassed the Quirk Singularity? Hopefully by now you understand what I'm saying. Okay, All Might is that person. That's why All Might is able to use One For All. Okay, makes sense. All right, let me make sense of it real quick.

Even when you look at the explanation that All Might gives about how he transforms from his Small Might form to his All Might form, he says it's like when you're sucking in your gut at the pool. That isn't really even a valid explanation, because he's saying it's like doing that, he's not saying that it is doing that, which are two separate things. But even if that's the case, it still leaves the blatant fact that he's transforming from a small form to a larger form.

## All Might's Parents

So what is my explanation for how All Might is able to do this without a Quirk? The answer is relatively simple. The one thing we know for sure, confirmed, is that All Might is Quirkless. But the one thing that Horikoshi has not revealed at all, for some reason, is who in the world All Might's parents are.

Now, whether he reveals them or not doesn't matter, but what does matter is that his parents played a huge role in him being who he is today. It is my belief that although All Might was born Quirkless, he wasn't born with a regular biological structure. Instead, he was born with a body that has evolved to a level surpassing every other person in My Hero Academia, granting him at least the ability to transform his body from a smaller form to a larger form. I know that sounds crazy, because you would think that that would require a Quirk, and you would be valid for having those beliefs a year and a half, two years ago.

## Shigaraki Proves It

But ever since the final war of My Hero Academia, it is 100% possible for All Might to transform his body without having a Quirk. [Shigaraki](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Tomura_Shigaraki), in his battle against the heroes, is in a similar predicament to the one he was in in the [Paranormal Liberation War arc](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Paranormal_Liberation_War_Arc), where he's essentially getting jumped by everyone and he isn't allowed to use his Quirk. That's due to [Monoma](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Neito_Monoma) copying [Aizawa](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Shota_Aizawa)'s [Erasure](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Erasure) Quirk, effectively erasing Shigaraki's Quirk, meaning Shigaraki is Quirkless against the heroes. And yet still, due to the fact that Shigaraki is 100% done with the genetic modifications that Dr. Ujiko and All For One put on his body to supersede the Quirk Singularity, his body has evolved to the point where he can mutate his body without having a Quirk.

So a physical mutation without having a Quirk has been demonstrated by two separate people. Shigaraki, who coincidentally got his body through artificial means, and All Might, who got his body through natural means. So all I'm claiming with this theory is that All Might was born from parents who likely had Quirks, and either his father or mother had a mutant body with a transformation Quirk. When All Might was conceived, he was granted the natural ability to physically mutate, in this case transform, his body, which ultimately allowed him to supersede the powers of One For All, effectively proving that he surpassed the Quirk Singularity.
