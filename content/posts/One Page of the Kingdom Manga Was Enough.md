## Intro
![kingdom Ch. 1, Pg. 34](image/kingdom-ch1-pg34.png)
This may look like a random page from the manga Kingdom, but to me, this page tells a story, one of friendship, commitment, and change. Two friends facing each other for what may be the final time.

## Shin and Piao

[Shin](https://kingdom.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Xin) and [Piao](https://kingdom.fandom.com/wiki/Li_Piao) are war orphans. Their parents were killed in war, and because of that, they now live with a Lydian. Now, Piao and Shin want to make a name for themselves, earning fame and fortune, and their plan is to harness their skill with their sword. They want to be skilled enough to scale the ladder of war, so that their names will resound across China as the greatest generals under the heavens.

But what they didn't know was that Piao would be getting a head start on their shared dream of becoming the greatest generals in China. One random day, a lord by the name of [Lord Changwen](https://kingdom.fandom.com/wiki/Shou_Bun_Kun) from the kingdom of [Qin](https://kingdom.fandom.com/wiki/Qin) came to the village. I looked it up, that's how you pronounce it. Lord Changwen came to the village specifically to buy Piao's freedom, offering him a position as an officer in the royal court.

Everyone was shocked, considering that Piao is essentially a slave to the Lydian, and the fact that becoming an officer in the royal court is skipping so many different steps. That's a warranted response. But what's fascinating is that Piao's immediate response to Lord Changwen is that Shin be granted a similar offer. Unfortunately, Piao's request was let down. Now he's met with a decision to either become an officer for the royal court or stay in the village.

## The Page

That brings us back to this page and this panel, where we see two boys standing face to face, prepared to duel. Years of memories, acquired skills, lessons learned, and their dreams cultivated together all led up to this moment. 1,253 matches. Piao is in the lead. 334 wins, 332 losses, and 587 ties. Just two points apart, to question if Shin is on par with Piao. And yet, we never see the conclusion. Oh my gosh.

But that is why this single page is so important, why it's so significant. I found it fitting that as we turn the page of this very pivotal moment, we see the beauty of a simple life from Shin's perspective. There's a wide shot turned to a close-up of Shin tilling the ground, indicating the diligence in his work. And the succession of panels shows Shin's interactions with the simplicities in life. Hearing the songs of the birds chirping, tasting the textures of a splendid cuisine, expressing the honor of assisting those in need. It's like the juxtaposition of Shin's grand desire to become the greatest general in China. This is Shin's new reality.

## Shin's Simple Life

It's only then that the author allows Shin to tell us that Piao has been going to the royal court for an entire month now. For me, in this moment, my mind was made up. Maybe Shin, in this environment, could be content with where he is. I mean, the commoners in the village already perceived that Shin has changed a lot since Piao left for the royal court. Or maybe something else would trigger Shin to pursue this grandiose goal he has, something that can lead Shin to the royal court or the military, where he can reunite with Piao in differing positions, but they can still aspire for the same goal. It turned out that I was halfway right and halfway wrong.

## The Rumor

Everything was normal until Shin overheard rumors that there was infighting in the royal court. This is where the plot thickens, so bear with me. Apparently, the king of Qin is too young to lead the throne. By proxy, the country is now led by a minister by the name of [Lu Buwei](https://kingdom.fandom.com/wiki/Ryo_Fui). And this is where the allegations begin.

The other ministers in the royal court aren't fond of Minister Lu' power in the kingdom. Therefore, they devised a plan with the king's younger brother to usurp the current king, under the governance that the other ministers would take power of the nation until the new king is of age to lead the kingdom. After a long time of preparation, the rumor is that the king's younger brother and his allies have begun the attack on the royal court. The same royal court that Piao is an officer in, which unfortunately implies that Piao may already be dead. And Shin snapped.

## Piao's Return

Seeing how devastated Shin was at the possibility that Piao was dead, I actually started to believe that Piao was alive, and that maybe this rumor would lead Shin to find his way to the royal court, where he would fight alongside his childhood friend and rival. But in the thick of the night, there was a knock on Shin's door. All Shin saw was a man lying in a trail of his own blood. That man was Piao, dying on the ground, with a mission for Shin to accomplish. This moment hit me hard, and it for sure hit Shin even harder.

I can't help but be reminded of this page. This moment in time where these two young warriors were facing off for a final time, with a declaration and vigor to their goals, and not knowing who won. Never being granted the confidence in seeing whether Shin was able to tie their record or if Piao won. Meaning Shin has to surpass him if he is to accomplish his side of their goal. And to see that Piao's life was unfortunately taken makes the simple world that Shin was living in that much bigger, making the questions that much stronger. How will Shin fare in this grand world?

I have to acknowledge Piao's dedication to come all the way from the royal court to his original poor village just to give Shin the opportunity to fulfill both of their dreams. That showcases that Piao's loyalty to his friend and the loyalty he has for his dream and goal are one and the same. That one cannot be done or fulfilled without the other. It's even more beautiful knowing that he will never be able to accomplish his goals, and his final desire was to accompany Shin in becoming the greatest under the heavens.

## Closing Thoughts

A single page can change the meaning of a story, and a single story can increase the wisdom of a man. This is the first chapter of the manga Kingdom, and this is my first impression of the first chapter. Kingdom looks to be a special story for me to journey into. The author's ability to tell a story through its characters' expressions and body language is what makes most of the scenes so powerful. Seemingly everything matters in this story. Every interaction and every decision bleeds into future decisions and future interactions.

Kingdom currently has over 860 chapters. Although 800-plus chapters is daunting, I plan on treating this story with delicacy. And only because through a single chapter did Yasuhisa Hara, the mangaka of this brilliant story, express to me that delicacy, meticulosity, and curiosity are the only ways to absorb what they are trying to express in their story. So I will take this one panel, one page, one chapter at a time, and it'll be great to have you there with me.

Till next time, make sure you stand up and win your zone every single day.
