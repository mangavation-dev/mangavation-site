## What We Know About Inko

We don't know much about Inko besides her name, her Quirk, the husband who works overseas, and the son who is the main character of his own series, go figure. But Horikoshi isn't just a mangaka. He's very meticulous with a character's name, design, Quirk, and personality, and he builds them in parallel to the story.

## Nana, Kotaro, and All For One

Nana was a pro hero and the seventh user of One For All. She had a son named Kotaro Shimura, who grew up hating her and pro heroes. In chapter 253, Kotaro suggests Nana abandoned him to fight crime and protect strangers. But a letter she left behind says, "Your mom has to go fight a really bad man now. That bad man might try to do mean things to you, and that's why we can't be together anymore."

That bad man was All For One, and he still got info on Nana's family, eventually getting Tenko Shimura, better known as Shigaraki. So why would Nana abandon her son to protect him while staying in the same area fighting crime? That doesn't make sense to me. I believe she went somewhere All For One didn't know about to prepare for their final battle, and there she conceived Inko.

## The Timeline

We know from basic math that Nana died about eleven years after leaving Kotaro. Shigaraki is 20 now, and Kotaro was 32 when Shigaraki was 5, so Kotaro is 47. Based on Endeavor being a first year and All Might a third year at UA, Endeavor is 46, so All Might is 49. All Might was 18 when Nana lost to All For One, which puts Kotaro at 16, and in his flashbacks he looks about 4 or 5, which is 11 years before her death. The letter makes it sound like the fight came right after, but that would make All Might 17, so the final battle came later. Don't judge me, I put a lot of effort into this theory. Nana needed that time to train, but she couldn't do it in Japan.

## Why America?

I think Nana and Gran Torino, her side dude, went to America. After Nana died, Gran Torino said All Might was nowhere near strong enough to rival All For One, so he planned to train him in secret at UA and send him to America after graduation. But he also said All For One has eyes and ears everywhere. So why America and not somewhere else in Japan? Because All For One once ruled Japan, and Gran Torino knew America was free from his surveillance. How would he know that? Because he and Nana went there and made connections. Now tell me that doesn't make sense. I estimate she had Inko about a year after leaving Kotaro.

## Character Design

Design is where this theory usually gets debunked, since Inko and Nana have different eyes. But looks don't rule out kinship, since Shoto looks nothing like Endeavor and more like his mother, Rei. All Might himself said Inko's hairstyle reminds him of Nana's. Horikoshi has over 100 named characters with different designs, and he made two with similar ones. That's not a coincidence.

## Quirks

Inko's unnamed Quirk pulls small objects toward her. In chapter 256, we learn Nana has a gravitational-type Quirk called Float. I think Horikoshi is holding back its description because it would directly tie Nana to Inko. From All Might's backstory, I think Float repels whatever she touches, the opposite of Inko's Quirk.

## Names and Meanings

Inko's name breaks down in kanji to "pull," from gravitation, a direct reference to her Quirk. "Inko" is also Japanese for parakeet, and Midoriya means green valley. Nana's name means seven, and in kanji it means green veggies, with Shimura meaning ambitious village. A green valley is vegetative land too, and Midoriya is Inko's married name. So Horikoshi connected green and vegetation in Deku's and Shigaraki's last names, even through marriage.

Parakeets are monogamous, loyal, and affectionate, which fits Inko and her husband, who's been overseas for at least 12 of Deku's 16 years. They also learn any human language, so if Inko was from America, she'd have learned Japanese, or the other way around.

## Parallel Lives

Nana had two children five years apart, both born in July, nine days apart. Deku is the ninth user of One For All, which could be nothing, but I found it intriguing. Kotaro grew up hating his mother, while Inko grew up loving her and even took her hairstyle. Both of their kids were born Quirkless, and Deku and Shigaraki are four years apart.

All For One found out about Nana's family years after her death and planned to use them against All Might. But Inko was born in America, so Shigaraki was the only grandson he knew about, and he was targeted and given a Quirk. Both loved heroes, and both ended up under the [Symbol of Peace], All Might, and the Symbol of Evil, All For One. That's the parallel Horikoshi built, all stemming from Nana.
