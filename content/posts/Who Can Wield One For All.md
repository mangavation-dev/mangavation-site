## Intro

The number one theory when it comes to [One For All](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/One_For_All) is, what? If [All Might](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Toshinori_Yagi) never met [Deku](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Izuku_Midoriya), or if he just never gave him the [Quirk](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Quirk), who would be the best candidate for One For All, with the story implying that any person who wields the power of One For All will eventually die? Many people would say that this is a bad faith conversation, but I would beg to differ.

[Hikage Shinomori](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Hikage_Shinomori) dying of old age is not at all evidence for One For All not being able to be transferred to other Quirk users. Listen to me when I say this. I believe that One For All can be transferred to other heroes with Quirks. And there are three characters who, if they had One For All, would not die. You may not believe me unless you have some context, so I'm going to wait to reveal them, and I want to tell you about the One For All Quirk.

## The Origin of One For All

The One For All Quirk was a Quirk that was never supposed to exist. A long time ago, before Quirks began to manifest all around the world, a homeless prostitute with twins was impregnated and was due to have twins. The eldest had the ability to take and give Quirks, and the youngest had the ability to transfer their Quirk to others. Because their mother died giving birth, these two kids were left to fend for themselves.

As they grew older, the eldest child began to use his abilities to benefit himself. If anyone did anything that he did not like or did not want, he would use his [meta ability](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Quirk) to take them out or take their meta ability away. So the eldest son named himself [Shigaraki](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/All_For_One), and named his Quirk [All For One](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/All_For_One_(Quirk)). And he also named his younger brother [Yoichi](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Yoichi_Shigaraki), which represents the first possession that he was given in this life.

From a young age to young adulthood, All For One continued to do malicious acts for his own benefit. And Yoichi would often try to steer him in a different direction, a direction in which he uses his power for good. But All For One never listened, and always tried to get Yoichi to be a part of his team, to be on his side. However, the main issue was there was no sign from All For One that his younger brother had a Quirk. In fact, he actually believed that when they were born, and All For One was absorbing all the nutrients of his mother and his younger brother, he may have taken the ability that his brother was originally supposed to have.

## All For One and Yoichi

Fast forward to adulthood, and All For One's reign of terror has engulfed all of Japan. All For One was still offering a seat by his side to his younger brother Yoichi. But Yoichi would always deny him, every single time, claiming that what he's doing to people is manipulative and evil, and that he should be using his power for good. Yoichi told All For One that he would never be supportive of who he is and what he does, and that made All For One even more upset. He figured that maybe the reason his younger brother was so against him was because he never had the power and ability to fend for himself.

So All For One made an effort to find an ability that would not only allow Yoichi to have power, but also to grow that power over a sustained period of time. During this period of time, he was the legitimate demon lord. He was the most powerful man with the most influence and the most destructive Quirk in all of Japan. And there were many vigilante groups that were created specifically for taking down All For One. One such group had two special individuals within it, [Kudo](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Toshitsugu_Kudo) and [Bruce Lee](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Bruce_Lee).

## Kudo, Bruce, and the Will

When All For One was away, they planned on raiding his quarters. That is where they found a locked-up Yoichi Shigaraki, looking completely helpless and malnourished. Even though they knew that this was the brother of All For One, Kudo decided to reach out his hand and save Yoichi. When All For One found this out, he ended up tracking them down and chasing them to get his brother back. But in his effort to get his brother back, he ended up killing Yoichi. The thought that he killed his brother stunned him so much that it allowed the other vigilantes, namely Kudo and Lee, to get away.

Later that day, while holding the remaining hand of his younger brother, All For One realized that he didn't feel a Quirk when he killed his younger brother. At this point in time, he is so proficient at using his Quirk that any person he touches, he would notice whether they have a [Quirk factor](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Quirk#Quirk_Factors) or not. And his little brother didn't have a Quirk factor. In that same exact time, Lee and Kudo did experiments, because Kudo felt something different about his body. It turned out that Yoichi had an original Quirk, and that Quirk transferred. But more importantly, the will of Yoichi transferred to Kudo.

This did two things. It started the crystalline network of Will, whose goal was to take down All For One. And it started a journey for All For One to get his brother back. This led to All For One tracking down Kudo and killing him. But Kudo must have known somehow that All For One was going to track him down and kill him, so he transferred the Quirk to the third user of One For All, Bruce Lee. In a similar fashion to his predecessor, All For One tracked down Bruce Lee to kill him.

## Shinomori

All For One tracked down and killed every single user of One For All besides three. All Might, who put a stop to him, Deku, who defeated him, and the fourth user of One For All, Shinomori, who ran away from him. Shinomori knew that he was unable to defeat All For One, so his plan was simple. He went all the way to an undisclosed, unlocatable location, and from there his sole goal was to cultivate the power within One For All as high as he possibly could. He had no idea what this would create.

But eventually he would find out that the stresses within One For All going up against the Quirk that he already had, the power was so strong that it began to stress him out physically, making him die of old age. When this was originally shown in My Hero Academia, it seemingly debunked the idea that One For All can be transferred to anyone without a Quirk. And I genuinely think that people only believe that because the next statement afterwards was telling Deku that he will be the last user of One For All. But I'm here to tell you that not only do those two things not go together, Shinomori dying of old age is not at all evidence for One For All not being able to be transferred to other Quirk users. And I can undeniably prove this.

## Quirk Factors and Will

But first, you need to understand a little bit about Quirk factors. A Quirk factor is the specific genetic and physical traits that compose your Quirk. To make it less complicated, let's look at [Bakugo](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Katsuki_Bakugo), for example. Bakugo has a combination of his mother's nitroglycerin skin and his father's acid sweat, but his Quirk factor is specifically in the palms of his hands. Nowhere else on his body are Quirk factors present, not until he has a Quirk awakening, in which his entire body is physically and genetically altered in order for the Quirk to exist. Every person has a different type of Quirk, and therefore every person has a different type of Quirk factor.

We actually found out that a Quirk factor copies a piece of your conscience, or your soul, within it. In other words, when you combine two Quirks together, you're ultimately combining two consciences. Based on evidence in My Hero Academia, there are only two things that can happen when two consciences come together. Number one is that they join together well. Number two, they rebel.

[Spinner](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Shuichi_Iguchi) in the final war of My Hero Academia. Every human that was created before the [Nomu](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Nomu), and All For One, is another example of the Quirks rebelling within his Quirk. But now wait, One For All is a Quirk that is made up of two Quirks, and it didn't rebel. All For One is a Quirk where he can steal Quirks, and they don't rebel, until they did. [Lady Nagant](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Kaina_Tsutsumi) was given a Quirk by All For One, and those two Quirks mesh well together. The only reason it exploded was because All For One placed something within the Quirk that would detonate if she decided to go a different route.

How can this be? How could any of this make sense? What can be the thing that would determine whether two Quirks mesh well together or whether they reject each other? It's the will of the user. It's the will of the user compared to the will of the other Quirk.

That's the reason Shinomori couldn't control One For All. That's the reason [Star and Stripe](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Cathleen_Bate) was able to rebel against All For One. That's the reason Lady Nagant's Quirk exploded. And that's the reason All For One felt confident in being able to steal One For All.

## All For One's Plan

The entire plan of All For One stealing One For All was predicated on one thing: having a stronger will than all the users of One For All combined, so that they may submit to whatever it is that his will says. But every time he's tried to take the Quirk, they've always rebelled. He's never had enough power. So what did he decide to do? He decided to manufacture a person who can have the will to take over and overthrow the One For All Quirk. If he was successful, then the One For All Quirk would be under him. But he didn't have enough will.

This is further proven when [Shigaraki](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Tomura_Shigaraki) stole the Quirk [New Order](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/New_Order). Right before Shigaraki stole Star and Stripe's Quirk, Star gave a rule for her conscience, which was her Quirk factor that was inside of All For One, which says that her Quirk will rebel against all other Quirks. And we get to see inside of All For One's world, Star and Stripe's conscience, her will, fighting against all the Quirks inside of All For One, and All For One himself.

And finally, the perfect example, Lady Nagant, where in her fight against Deku, she was unwavering 99.9% of the time. The moment that her will slipped was the moment that the Quirk that she had within her overtook her and exploded. The same thing was true for Shigaraki in the final war. It all points to the strength of your will.

Hikage Shinomori's will is the weakest of every single user of One For All. How strong is your will? If the only thing you do when you receive this power is run away, because you know you're incapable of beating this person, and try to strengthen your Quirk, your will is not that strong. That means the power of One For All will be stronger than your own conscience, and thus he was unable to wield the Quirk, because the Quirk was ultimately wielding him.

## Three Candidates

I believe that One For All can be transferred to other heroes with Quirks, under the caveat that they have immensely strong will. Similar to the will of Star and Stripe, similar to the will of the Quirk that All For One gave to Lady Nagant, similar to the will of Shigaraki preventing All For One from taking it, and then losing his body to All For One as well. Now I want to speak on three characters whom I already know would have One For All and completely take it to a different level.

The first is the number one hero in all of Japan, [Lemillion](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Mirio_Togata). Not only would he be able to have the will to sustain One For All, he would master One For All so much faster than anybody has been able to master it. And with his Quirk, [Permeation](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Permeation), he quite literally would be the most invincible hero in the history of the world. But he wouldn't be the strongest, because the second person that I believe can wield One For All is the strongest hero in the world currently.

That is [Shoto Todoroki](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Shoto_Todoroki), with 10-plus years of mastering [Phosphor](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Phosphor), having the hottest firepower in the entire world, having likely the coldest ice power in the entire world, and then adding the powers of One For All on top of that. Shoto would be unequivocally the most destructive hero in the entire world. And we're not even going to talk about adding [Fa Jin](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Fa_Jin) to [Flashfire Fist](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Flashfire_Fist), [Prominence Burn](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Prominence_Burn), Phosphor. That's just insane.

And the third person, who would be perfect for One For All because he already had it, is Great Explosion Murder God Dynamight. An awakened Bakugo with 10-plus years of experience that also has One For All would probably be the only hero in the history of the world that can quite literally save anyone. I'm thinking about the sheer speed he would have, on top of having [Gearshift](https://myheroacademia.fandom.com/wiki/Gearshift), on top of having Fa Jin. I don't think we can grasp just how much distance Bakugo could travel in a split second if he had One For All.
